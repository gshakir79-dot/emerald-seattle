Beyond the City — 3.5–4 HR DRIVE · OVERNIGHT RECOMMENDED

Seabrook

Nothing about Seabrook existed before 2004 — it's a planned New Urbanist beach town built deliberately walkable, with a car-free square, pastel cottages on rocking-chair porches, and the entire wide, flat Washington coast at the end of every street.

The Plan

7:00

Leave early

The drive runs I-5 to Olympia, then US-12/SR-8 to Aberdeen, then north on SR-109 through Hoquiam to the coast. Gas and food are sparser the closer you get — top off in Aberdeen.

11:00

The town square

Seabrook's entire commercial core is car-free: a green ringed by shops, a bakery, and the deliberately absurd 'Curse of Pirate Death' mini golf course.

12:30

The beach

Flat, wide, and dog-friendly, running for miles in both directions. Kites, driftwood forts, and low-tide agate hunting are the entire activity list, and that's the point.

14:30

Lunch in town

A handful of small restaurants and a market cover the basics — nothing fancy, everything walkable from wherever you're staying.

16:00

Griffiths-Priday State Park

A short walk north of town where the Copalis River meets the ocean — dunes, driftwood, and far fewer people than the main beach.

Need to know

  • Do the math before committing to a single day: it's roughly 7–8 hours of driving round trip. An overnight in one of Seabrook's rental cottages changes the trip entirely.
  • The town center is genuinely car-free — expect to park at a peripheral lot and walk in with your bags.
  • Summer weekends book cottages out well in advance; this isn't a spontaneous overnight in July.
  • Amenities thin out fast past Aberdeen — fill the tank and grab any supplies you need before the last stretch on SR-109.

Common questions

How far is Seabrook, WA from Seattle?

About 3.5 to 4 hours by car, driving through Olympia and Aberdeen before heading north on SR-109 to the coast — the longest drive of any trip on this list.

Is Seabrook a real town or was it built recently?

It's a planned community built starting in 2004 in a deliberately walkable, New Urbanist style, with a car-free town square and cottage rentals rather than a historic town center.

Is Seabrook better as a day trip or an overnight?

Overnight, honestly. At 7–8 hours of round-trip driving, a single day leaves very little time on the beach — renting one of the town's cottages for a night changes the math completely.
